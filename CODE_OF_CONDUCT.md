# Code of conduct

Tessaly follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give feedback on the work and not the person, and do not harass anyone. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, chat and any space run by the project.

## Reporting

Email hello@tessaly.dev with what happened, where, and links or screenshots if you have them. The maintainers will read every report, keep the reporter's identity private, and reply within 5 working days.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and warn, suspend or ban contributors who break this code. The enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant 2.1 apply.
